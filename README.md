# Vive v Vive

**VIVE V VIVES MUST LIVE ON.**

Our League of Legends 5v5 customs, run like a royal court. The app splits the teams, deals lanes and champions,
runs the bets and the chaos, keeps the stats, and roasts whoever deserves it. Everything updates live: you never
need to refresh.

Site: https://vive-v-vive.vercel.app

---

## Start in one minute

1. **Sign in with Discord** (top right).
2. **Claim your player**: profile menu, then *Claim your player*. This links your account to your name in the roster,
   so your wins, titles and points count.
3. **Join the queue** if you want in on the next game (lobby, *The Queue*).
4. Wait for the host to roll the teams. Your screen follows the game by itself.

Every button with a small **i** next to it explains what it does and what it costs. Hover it on a computer, tap it on
your phone.

## How a game works

A game goes through these steps. The bar at the top of the home page always shows where you are.

```
Lobby  ->  Draft  ->  Royal Decrees  ->  Bets  ->  Live  ->  Result + Tribunal
                       (90 seconds)    (5 minutes)          (5 minute vote)
```

### 1. Lobby

The host picks the ten players.

- **The Queue**: first come, first served. Join at the back, leave any time, even during a game. You leave the line
  when you are dealt into a game (a canceled game does not give the spot back), when you are benched, or after 12
  hours. With winner stays on, the winners keep their seats, the front of the line takes the losers' places, and
  losers who rotate out go to the back.
- **The Roster**: tap ten names. *Seat the queue* seats the first ten in line (your own picks fill any empty seats).
  *Same ten as last game* does what it says.
- **Roll the teams**: splits the ten into Blue and Red, deals each player a lane and a random champion.
- **Coach draft** (optional): two coaches pick their players one by one instead of random teams.

### 2. Draft

Everyone watches the reveal at the same time: lanes first, then the slot machine deals each champion.

- **Re-roll** (host): new champions for everyone who is not locked.
- **Padlock on a card** (host): locks that player's champion so a re-roll keeps it.
- **Start game** (host): opens the Royal Decrees.
- Tap any player card to see their champion, lane, title and profile.

### 3. Royal Decrees (90 seconds)

Spend points to mess with the draft before anyone loads in. Each decree has an info bubble with its exact effect.

| Decree | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The King's Wager | 49 | Bet your own team wins. Pays 2x, and each hostile decree on your side after you wager adds +0.5x (capped at 2.5x). Up to 3 per game. |
| Royal Pardon | 59 | Protects one player: other decrees cannot touch their lane or champion this game. |
| Royal Makeover | 59 | Rerolls your own champion. Always a different one. |
| Exile a Champion | 75 | Bans someone's champion for this game. They roll a new one. |
| Usurp a Seat | 89 | You and a teammate swap lanes and champions. They do not get a vote. |
| Forced March | 99 | Two players on the same team swap lanes, keeping their champions. |
| Musical Thrones | 119 | A whole team swaps seats, lanes and champions. Nobody keeps their own. |
| The Purge | 139 | A whole team gets brand new champions. |

Most decrees are once per game per player. If the game is canceled, every decree is refunded.

### 4. Bets (5 minutes)

**Royal Wagers** (the main bet)

- Pick Blue or Red, pick an amount, press bet. Odds move live as people bet: the side with less money pays more.
- **Early bird**: bets in the first 60 seconds earn +10% extra profit if they win.
- **All-in**: bet your whole balance (100 or more) for +15% extra profit. Cannot be insured.
- **Raise**: you can add more on the same side until bets lock. You cannot switch sides.
- **Guardian Angel insurance**: pay a premium (about a third of your stake, more on long shots). If you lose, you get
  60% of your stake back. If you win, the premium is gone.
- Players in the game can only bet on their own team. Spectators bet on anyone.
- If nobody bets on one side, or the game is canceled, everyone gets their bet back.

**Prophecies** (one per game, 10 to 50 points)

- Call who will be voted **MVP** or the biggest **Inter**. If the Tribunal agrees with you, you split that pot with
  everyone who called it.

**Duels** (10 to 500 points)

- Challenge someone (or throw an open challenge): "I take Blue for 100". They take the other side.
- The winner takes the pot minus a 10% tithe. A 100-point duel pays the winner 190.
- Players can only duel for their own team. Unanswered challenges are refunded when bets lock.

### 5. Live

Go play. When the game is over, the host presses **Declare winner** and picks the side.

- **Lock bets now** and **Close decrees now** let the host skip a timer. Timers also close by themselves.
- **Cancel game** refunds every bet, insurance, decree, duel and prophecy.

### 6. Result and the Tribunal

- Winners get **+10**, losers **+2**. Bets, duels, wagers, bounties and title perks pay out automatically.
- **The Tribunal** opens for 5 minutes: everyone votes the **MVP** and the **Inter**. You cannot vote for yourself.
- The MVP gets +15. The Inter goes to the pillory for free, by decree of the Tribunal.

## Points

You start each act with a purse. You can go down to **-300** (debt) by signing the Salaxe pledge.

| How you get points | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Win a game | +10 |
| Play a game (loss) | +2 |
| Daily stipend (open the app) | +5 per day |
| **Sing His Praises** (solve the King's Riddle, then write a praise for Salaxe) | **+100 per day** |
| Sharp Mind: the King's Riddle solved on the first try | +20 (paid with the praise) |
| Salaxe crowns your praise | +50 |
| Voted MVP by the Tribunal | +15 |
| Winning bets, wagers, duels, prophecies, bounties | depends on the odds |
| Title perks after a game | small bonus, depends on your titles |

| How you spend points | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Bets, duels, prophecies | your stake |
| Guardian Angel insurance | about a third of your stake |
| Royal Decrees | 49 to 139 |
| A bounty on someone | 20 or more |
| The pillory (shame someone for 24 h) | 40 |
| Titles | 300 to 1,500 |

### Sing His Praises

Once a day, write a praise for Salaxe and get **100 points**. The rules: at least 8 words, between 40 and 280
characters, name him (Salaxe, SLX, Chklat, Chalaksse, Chklass, Goblino...), and write something original (no copy
paste). Salaxe can **Crown** a praise (+50 to the author) or **Scorn** it in public. The app reminds you once a day
at most, never during a game.

Before you can write, **the King tests your wit** with one small riddle (free):

- **The Royal Count**: a number sequence like 2, 4, 8, 16... pick the next number among four.
- **The Weathervane**: a row of arrows turning by a rule. Pick where the next one points.
- **The Royal Plumbing**: tap pipes to turn them until the water flows from the entry to the crown.

Solve it on the **first try** for the **Sharp Mind** bonus: **+20**, paid with your praise (120 instead of 100). A
wrong answer shows the solution and the King poses a new riddle, but the bonus is gone. Up to 10 riddles a day:
miss them all and there is no praise until midnight UTC.

## Titles

Titles change the game. Each one has an **effect** that works the moment you act (cheaper decrees, better insurance,
bigger praise...) plus a small **perk** after each game. Open a title's bubble to see exactly what it does.

| Tier | Price | Slots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legendary | 1,500 (15 hand kisses) | super rare (2 slots, shared with Epic) |
| Epic | 1,000 (10 hand kisses) | super rare |
| Rare | 600 (6 hand kisses) | bronze (3 slots) |
| Common | 300 (3 hand kisses) | basic (4 slots) |

A hand kiss is one written praise (100 points).

| Title | Tier | Effect, the moment you act |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Usurper | Legendary | +1 use of every limited Royal Decree per game |
| Kingslayer | Legendary | decrees aimed at Salaxe or his team cost 20% less |
| Copium Dealer | Legendary | Guardian Angel covers 75% of the stake instead of 60% |
| Last Loyalist | Epic | written praise pays +25% |
| The One Who Stopped Kneeling | Epic | your champion cannot be exiled |
| Delayed Victory Specialist | Epic | a lost duel gives back 20% of the stake |
| Crown Thief | Rare | early-conviction bet window lasts 90 s instead of 60 s |
| The Disrespector | Rare | bestowing a curse costs 20% less |
| The Holdout | Rare | Guardian Angel costs 25% less on Salaxe's side |
| Baron Caller (Derogatory) | Rare | King's Wager starts at x2.2 (still capped at x2.5) |
| El Goblino Pequeno | Rare | duel tithe halved |
| Main Character (Self-Diagnosed) | Rare | the Herald announces every bet, decree and duel you make |
| The 0/8 Powerspike | Rare | all-in bonus is +25% of the profit instead of +15% |
| Still Kneeling | Common | daily stipend doubled |
| Throne Polisher | Common | Royal Favor pays +50% |
| Court Jester (curse) | Legendary | bets capped at 50 points per game |
| The Crawling One (curse) | Epic | no Guardian Angel insurance |
| Boot Licker (curse) | Rare | your decrees cost 20% more |
| Royal Carpet (curse) | Rare | decrees aimed at you cost others 20% less |
| Salaxe's Emotional Support (curse) | Common | your daily stipend goes to Salaxe |

The decree and devotion milestone titles also stack small bonuses (decrees -3/-5/-7%, praise +5/+5/+10%).

- **Loadout**: wear up to 9 titles at once and build combos: 2 super rare slots (Legendary or Epic), 3 bronze slots
  (Rare) and 4 basic slots (Common). Two pairs never mix (Copium Dealer and The Holdout, El Goblino Pequeno and
  Delayed Victory Specialist), and rebels and loyalists cannot share the super rare slots. Every effect has a cap
  (decree discounts stop at 30% off, praise at +40%, coverage at 75%), and all your post-game perks together pay at
  most 60 points a game. Your loadout is public: friends see your build on your profile ("See their strategy").
- **Curses**: mockery titles (Court Jester, Boot Licker...) are bought for someone else. They hurt the wearer for 8
  games, take no slot and pay the person who bought them. The victim can buy back their dignity for exactly the
  curse's price.
- Free titles come from milestones (games played, wins, bets, praises...) and act awards.
- Your titles show on your player card during the draft and the game, next to your name everywhere, and in the live
  feed.

## Bounties and the pillory

- **Bounty**: put 20+ points on a player's head. Others can add to it. If that player loses, the linked players on the
  winning team split it. If they win, the bounty grows. Unclaimed bounties are refunded after 7 days.
- **Pillory**: 40 points to put a player on the wall of shame for 24 hours with a roast (the app suggests one from their
  worst stats). Anyone can throw a free egg to keep them up one more hour.

---

## Everything else

- **Live updates**: toasts tell you what other people just did (bets, decrees, duels...). The dot in the top bar shows
  you are live. Its menu holds notifications and sound (both on by default), quiet mode and vibration.
- **Notifications**: tap *Turn on notifications* once and your phone or computer tells you when a game is launched,
  decrees or bets open, the game goes live, the result lands or a game is canceled, plus anything aimed at you (a duel,
  a bounty, a curse, the pillory, your praise verdict, your pick as a coach). It works with the app minimized, and
  with it closed when you are signed in. On iPhone, install the app first (Share, then *Add to Home Screen*), open it
  from the Home Screen, then allow notifications.
- **Profiles**: tap any name to see that player's season, titles, recent games, best duo and arch-nemesis.
- **Stats by season**: stats, rivalries, duos and history show the current act by default. Use the season picker for
  older acts or all time. Tap a game in a rivalry to see the full match.
- **Leaderboard, Gamba hall, Archives**: who is rich, who is broke, who gambled it all, and how past acts ended.
- **Install it**: on your phone, use *Add to Home Screen* for a full-screen app.

## For the host

| Button | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roll the teams | Lobby | Deals teams, lanes and champions for the ten selected players |
| Re-roll | Draft | New champions for everyone not locked |
| Padlock | Draft | Keeps a player's champion through re-rolls |
| Start game | Draft | Opens the 90-second decree window |
| Close decrees now | Decrees | Skips to bets |
| Lock bets now | Bets | Closes betting, the game is live |
| Declare winner | Live | Records the result and pays everyone |
| Cancel game | Any time before the result | Refunds everything |

If *Declare winner* says the settlement is incomplete, press it again: nothing is lost and nothing is paid twice.

## For developers

- Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, shadcn/ui on Base UI, Supabase (Postgres, Auth with
  Discord, Realtime), TanStack Query, motion.
- Run locally: `pnpm install` then `pnpm dev` (needs `.env.local` with `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`).
- Database changes live in `supabase/`. After a deploy, run `supabase/phase10_all.sql` in the Supabase SQL editor
  (safe to run again), check the site, then run `supabase/phase11_lockdown.sql` last.
- Web Push (notifications with the app closed): run `supabase/phase10_push.sql` (any time, before or after the
  lockdown) and set `NEXT_PUBLIC_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` and `VAPID_SUBJECT` (`mailto:...`) in Vercel,
  then redeploy. Generate a pair with `npx web-push generate-vapid-keys`. Without them push is off and everything
  else works. Optional `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (server only) also covers phase changes that a signed-out visitor
  happens to trigger.
- Pushing to `main` deploys to Vercel.
